Client Types

  • Expected to Work (ETW)
    Clients assessed as able to participate in employment activities. Subdivided by working status, availability, or temporary unavailability. Generally expected to pursue employment and may receive income supplementation for low earnings.
  • Barriers to Full Employment (BFE)
    Clients facing significant, often long-term, barriers that preclude full-time employment — including chronic health conditions (>6 months), caregiving responsibilities, or other life circumstances. Generally receive higher benefit levels.

Raw File Format

The CSV uses a two-row header pattern:

  • Row 1 (title): merged label — Alberta Assisted Living and Social Services · Income Support Caseload
  • Row 2 (columns): Ref_Date, Geography, Measure Type, Measure, Value + 9 trailing empty columns
  • Rows 3+ (data): One observation per row — a single (month × measure type × measure) combination
